```html
<meta name="rating" content="general" />
```

**Why Important:**
- **theme-color:** Better PWA experience
- **apple-mobile-web-app-capable:** Improved iOS experience
- **robots:** Allows Google to preview images and snippets (better SERP appearance)
- **revisit-after:** Tells crawlers to revisit frequently
- **rating:** Indicates content is suitable for general audiences
